# Ambros' Journey
### CMS 484 Capstone • Two Kaleys and a Dylan

A 2D educational adventure about using AI safely. Ambros is a middle schooler who gets pulled into a game through his laptop. To return home, he must complete missions and defeat the AI bosses across Spring, Summer, Fall and Winter.

**Completed:** the opening story video and the first playable Spring season. Spring includes movement, a following camera, the Ancient Mage tutorial, three misinformation bat missions, and a final guardian quiz. Scoring at least 80% unlocks the Summer gate and adds the Mage to Ambros' party.

**Next:** Summer, Fall, Winter and the final AI safety challenge. The Summer gate is unlocked by Spring progress, but Summer's playable content is not built yet.

Open the inner `capstone` folder in Unity **6000.6.2f1**. Choose **Capstone → Play From Opening**, or **Play Spring Only**. Move with **WASD / arrow keys**, interact with **E**, and answer with **1 / 2**. Progress currently lasts for the play session.
