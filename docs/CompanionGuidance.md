# Companion guidance

Guides speak in a small parchment caption while the player keeps walking. Each caption gets 8–12 seconds of visible reading time. Lessons, the journal, pause, portal effects and videos hide it and pause its timer. The caption shares the season's canvas, font and parchment art and cannot receive mouse clicks.

- Spring's controls guide leads to the Blossom Grove field note. Closing it automatically completes the Mage orientation; the field notes and myth bats still require their own interactions and answers. Older saves from before the Mage conversation receive the same orientation without losing progress.
- Summer's Warriors and Fall's Emberfist begin their escort automatically. They wait within five world units while leading. Even a long frame cannot move them beyond that waiting radius: movement is clipped along their planned route, with collision checks retained. Their arrival tips explain the first station without opening a quiz. The player presses E at the station when ready. Reaching that station before the guide also completes the escort instruction; the guide finishes walking there without making the player return.
- Winter's arrival advice is passive. Closing the Warden victory message automatically enables cave guidance. Older saves with a defeated Warden receive that guidance on their next free frame. The player still has to walk into the cave and finish its lesson before the finale.
- Passive NPC name cards have no E prompt and do not compete with nearby field notes, artifacts or bosses for interaction. Lesson notes and objectives retain the instructions; Summer's learning notes are available through J even before a ward is completed.

The interaction controls and quiz rules are unchanged. Field notes and bosses remain deliberate E interactions. Wrong field-note answers can be tried again; boss answers are recorded once per attempt.

`CompanionGuidanceChecks` verifies movement remains available, captions reuse the existing canvas and do not intercept clicks, timer suspension during other screens, automatic expiration, escort arrival without opening a lesson, migration of earlier saves without changing saved coordinates, actual guidance text fit, full source/destination fade lifetimes, and cave guidance after victory. The seasonal presentation and boss-exit checks have been updated for the automatic introductions. The final November 21 native integration passed with the escort, caption and slow-frame waiting checks included.
